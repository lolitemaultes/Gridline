# Browser tabs

The browser's tabs (All, Project, Plugins, Sounds and the star for Starred) are yours to arrange.
Add your own folders as tabs, close the tabs you never use, rename and reorder them. Gridline keeps
your tabs for you (per user, in `browser-tabs.txt` in Gridline's settings folder), so they are the
same in every project.

## Adding a tab

- Click **+** at the end of the tabs and choose **Folder as a tab...** to pick a folder. Its
  contents show as a list of their own when you click the tab.
- The same menu lists your browser folders (the ones added with **Add folder...**) that have no tab
  yet, so one click makes a tab of any of them.
- Or right-click a folder anywhere in the browser and choose **Open as a tab**.

The files in a folder tab are searched like the rest of the browser, and you can rename, move and
delete them there as in your browser folders.

## Closing, renaming and moving tabs

- Right-click a tab for **Rename tab...** (folder tabs), **Move left**, **Move right** and
  **Close tab**. A middle-click closes a tab too.
- Drag a tab sideways to move it.
- A built-in tab you closed comes back from the **+** menu, under **Closed tabs**, in its usual
  place.
- The browser always keeps at least one tab.

## In the Clips and Smooth layouts

Those layouts show a list of places instead of tabs. Your folder tabs are listed there as places
of their own, under **On this computer**. Right-click a place to rename it or remove it, or to add
another folder as a place. A folder added in one layout shows in the others too.

## In the Tracker

The Tracker draws the Browser in its own look, beside the pattern: the tabs are bevelled buttons
with the shown one lit, like the Tracker's page tabs, and the **+** sits at the right end of the
strip. A tab always shows its whole name: when the tabs do not fit in one row, they go on to a
second. The tag chips are grey bevels, the one you picked lit. Everything works as in Studio.

## Hiding folders

Right-click any folder in the browser, in any tab including your own folder tabs, and choose
**Hide folder** to take it out of the way. A hidden folder and everything inside it leave the
browser: its rows, and its files in search results and in Starred.

While any folder is hidden, the header has an eye button. Click it to show hidden folders again
for a while: they come back dimmed, with a crossed out eye at the right of the row. To bring a
folder back for good, click that eye, or right-click the folder and choose **Show folder**.

Hiding a folder hides the folders inside it too. Showing it again also shows any folder inside
that you hid on its own, so nothing stays hidden where you cannot see it.

The hidden folders are kept for you (per user, in `browser-hidden.txt` in Gridline's settings
folder), not in the project, and are the same in every layout: Studio, Clips, Smooth and the
Tracker.
