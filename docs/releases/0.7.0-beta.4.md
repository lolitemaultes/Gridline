**Gridline 0.7.0 beta 4 is a beta.** It is published as a GitHub Pre-release, so Gridline's update check only offers it to people who turn on **Include beta versions** in **Settings, General, Updates**. Anyone can download it from this page, and 0.7.0 itself follows later. Projects from 0.6.1 and the earlier betas open as they are. If something does not work as it should, please [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/lolitemaultes/Gridline/issues); every report helps get 0.7.0 ready.

Beta 4 makes big projects open without freezing, behind a **loading window** that shows what is happening. **Patterns can hold automation clips**, and **Platter performances** are now recorded as automation clips you can cut and play from anywhere. **Tideline** follows a mixer track, a channel or MIDI that you pick in its own window. Many fixes land as well: clip pitch, Rubber Band start-up, tempo detection for drum and bass, and FL Studio project import and export. Collaborative projects need everyone on the same version, so for this beta everyone in a session runs 0.7.0 beta 4.

## Highlights

### Projects open without freezing

Opening a project no longer stops Gridline while samples and plugins load.

- **A loading window** takes over the moment you pick a project: its name, a progress bar that counts every sample and plugin, what is loading right now (which plugin is starting, 3 of 9) and a log of everything loaded with its time, so a slow plugin is easy to spot.
- **Warnings** for missing samples, plugins that are not installed and stand-ins are listed in the window and stay on a notification card afterwards.
- **Cancel** (or Esc) goes back to the project you had open, exactly as it was. A project that cannot be read says why in the same window.
- Quick projects open without the window flashing up. It works the same for Gridline projects, FL Studio projects, zipped projects and collaborative projects.

### Automation clips inside patterns

- **A pattern can hold automation clips.** They play wherever the pattern plays and are edited in the automation editor like any other clip: points, curve shapes, the LFO tool, a name and a colour.
- **Add one** from a control's right-click menu (Create automation clip in pattern), from the channel rack's or the pattern's menu, or move or copy a playlist automation clip into the pattern.
- **See them on the playlist.** A pattern's clips in the playlist draw the curves over the notes, and the View menu can show notes only or automation only. Double-click a curve to edit it. The channel rack lists them as rows below the channels, and the browser under Current project.
- Changing a pattern's pitch wheel or volume no longer needs the piano roll's event lane. Pattern lanes from older projects open as clips and sound exactly the same.

### Platter passes as automation clips

- **Every pass becomes named automation clips**, one per control you used, such as "Platter A wheel": the record's position, play, the wheel and its touch, the mouse, loops, reverse and pitch bend, and every fader and knob. In song mode they are a grouped set of clips in the playlist; in pattern mode they are clips of a new pattern, so the wheel shows moving on the pattern's playlist clips.
- **Play from anywhere.** The clips hold the decks' state, so a pass plays from wherever playback starts: cut its clips, trim their start or move them and the decks pick the pass up there.
- Left as recorded, a pass still replays exactly as played, at any buffer size, and passes recorded as notes in beta 3 keep playing as before.
- A pass recorded in song mode is one pass, even on a short or empty song: recording without a time selection plays on past the song end instead of looping back and splitting the performance.
- **Tempo:** Platter and Seltmann read drum and bass at its real tempo (174 BPM, not 116 or 87), and Platter's decks get **/2** and **x2** buttons next to the load button for a track heard at half or double time.

### Tideline follows the project

- **Pick a source in Tideline's window.** In Audio mode choose a mixer track or a channel (audio clip channels too) from the new Source list, and Tideline makes the sidechain route for you; it shows in the mixer. A **Look-ahead** control (up to 10 ms) lets the duck land right on the hit while the project stays in time.
- **MIDI mode can follow a channel:** every note of its patterns and the playlist, notes you play live on it and each start of its audio clips, exactly on the sample, with no detection. It can also follow the MIDI input or MIDI Out ports, and a note filter takes one note or a range.
- MIDI triggers are now sample-accurate instead of firing at the start of the audio block.

### Fixes

- **Clip pitch keeps the clip.** Changing an audio clip's pitch on the playlist no longer changes its length or how its audio fills it, in every time mode. Switching a clip's time mode no longer resizes it, a trimmed clip stays trimmed when its time factor changes, and a resampled, stretched clip on a pitched channel renders at the pitch it plays at.
- **Rubber Band starts without crackles.** Notes in patterns and the playlist and audio clips do Rubber Band's start-up work in small pieces during the blocks before they play, so a chord or several clips on one beat no longer take several blocks' worth of CPU at once.
- **FL Studio 2026 projects** open with their whole playlist arrangement: pattern and audio clips with their lengths, offsets, fades and gain. An arrangement Gridline cannot fully read is reported instead of dropped quietly.
- **Slicex chops arrive in Kerf exactly:** every region in its order, on its key, with its own start, end and name, and they go back out the same way when you export to the FL Studio format.
- **Third-party plugins such as Serum 2** export to the FL Studio format with their original plugin host settings, and a plugin that was played by a stand-in is written back as the original instrument with its name.

The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/lolitemaultes/Gridline/blob/v0.7.0-beta.4/CHANGELOG.md).

## Known limits

- Half-time grooves may still read at half speed in Platter and Seltmann; the /2 and x2 buttons put them right.
- Notes played live on a Rubber Band instrument still start the moment you play them, so their start-up work is not spread out ahead.
- Collaborative projects use Gridline's own plugins only, and everyone in a session needs 0.7.0 beta 4.

## Upgrading

- Projects from 0.6.1 and the earlier betas open as they are. Pattern automation lanes open as automation clips inside their patterns, and Platter takes and note-based passes keep playing.
- Audio clips from earlier versions whose pitch was changed fill their clip again, as described above. Imported FL Studio projects keep their original timing.
- Tracks analysed by Platter or Seltmann before are analysed again.

## Install

| System | File |
| --- | --- |
| macOS 11 or later (Apple Silicon and Intel) | `Gridline-0.7.0-beta.4-macos-universal.dmg` |
| Windows 10 / 11, x64 | `Gridline-0.7.0-beta.4-windows-x64-setup.exe` or `-windows-x64.zip` |
| Windows 10 / 11, ARM64 | `Gridline-0.7.0-beta.4-windows-arm64-setup.exe` or `-windows-arm64.zip` |
| Linux x86_64 | `.AppImage`, `-linux-amd64.deb`, `-linux-x86_64.rpm` or `-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |
| Linux aarch64 | `.AppImage`, `-linux-arm64.deb`, `-linux-aarch64.rpm` or `-linux-aarch64.tar.gz` |
| FreeBSD, OpenBSD, NetBSD (x86_64) | `-freebsd-x86_64.tar.gz`, `-openbsd-x86_64.tar.gz`, `-netbsd-x86_64.tar.gz` |

- **macOS:** open the `.dmg` and drag Gridline to Applications. The first time, open it with right-click, then Open.
- **Windows:** run the `-setup.exe`. If SmartScreen appears, choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
- **Linux:** use the `.AppImage`, `.deb`, `.rpm` or `.tar.gz`.
- **BSDs:** use the `.tar.gz` for your system.

Every file's checksum is in `Gridline-0.7.0-beta.4-SHA256SUMS`.
