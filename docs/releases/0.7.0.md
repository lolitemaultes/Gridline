**Gridline 0.7.0 is here.** It is the biggest Gridline release so far and brings together everything from the four 0.7.0 betas and the weeks since. Two or more people can now **make music together**, live, on one project. Gridline's own instruments and effects run in any DAW as the **Gridline Suite**. **Platter** puts two DJ decks and a mixer on a channel, and **Seltmann** chops any audio you drop on it. Around them: Plasma, stem splitting, Import from URL, a reworked Kerf, clips that follow the tempo, smoother motion and many fixes. Projects from 0.6.1 and from every beta open as they are. Gridline's update check offers 0.7.0 to everyone on 0.6.x and on a 0.7.0 beta.

## Highlights

### Make music together, live

Open the Collaboration panel, give the project a name and press **Make collaborative**, then invite people with a one-time code that expires after 15 minutes to 24 hours. They paste it in **File, Join collaborative project...** and choose where their copy of the project goes. From then on channels, notes, the playlist, the mixer, automation, Gridline's plugin settings and audio files sync in a fraction of a second, and everyone keeps their own windows and transport.

- **Peer to peer, no server and no account.** Computers connect directly on the same network, through routers (hole punching and automatic port opening), through another collaborator, or through an optional TURN relay. **Test my connection** reports what your network allows.
- **Private by design.** Everything is end-to-end encrypted, every change is signed by its author, and safety codes let you check who you are talking to. Nothing goes anywhere but to the people you invited and the STUN or TURN server you configure.
- **See each other work.** Collaborators' cursors point at the same track, note, step, knob or mixer strip on every screen, whatever the window size, zoom or interface scale, and changes flash in their author's colour. People show in the top bar with their picture, an online lamp and **Follow their playhead**.
- **Sound stays on your computer.** Playback, previews, held keys, solo and recording are never shared, and a collaborator's edits never make sound on yours.
- **Work offline.** The project saves itself continuously and stays a normal project folder. Changes travel the next time two computers that have them are online together, and edits made to the same thing while apart go to the admin's **Sync review**. Undo takes back only your own changes.
- **The Collaborative view** on the welcome screen joins with a code, starts or shares a project, and lists every collaborative project on this computer with its members, your role, what is waiting for you and who is working on it right now.

The full guide is in [docs/collaboration.md](https://github.com/lolitemaultes/Gridline/blob/v0.7.0/docs/collaboration.md).

### The Gridline Suite: Gridline's plugins in any DAW

Every download carries the **Gridline Suite**: 118 of Gridline's instruments and effects, each a plugin of its own for other hosts, as VST3, CLAP and (on macOS) Audio Unit. Each plugin is the same sound and the same editor as inside Gridline, with named, automatable parameters, the factory presets, a sidechain input where the effect uses one, and a state that moves between Gridline and another host unchanged.

**Gridline Suite Mini** adds 35 compact single-purpose effects in the same formats: tone, dynamics, drive, pitch, motion, time and utility, each with one clear job and a small editor.

The Windows installer and the Linux packages put both suites where hosts find them. On macOS, with the AppImage and with the `.tar.gz` builds, Gridline asks on first launch whether to copy them into your plug-in folders, and every new Gridline brings the copies to its own version. See [docs/gridline-suite.md](https://github.com/lolitemaultes/Gridline/blob/v0.7.0/docs/gridline-suite.md).

### Platter: two decks on a channel

Platter is a whole DJ setup as an instrument: deck A and deck B with a mixer between them.

- **Decks.** Drop a track on a deck and Platter finds its tempo and beat grid, then plays it in time with the project or with the other deck. Drum and bass reads at its real tempo, and **/2** and **x2** correct a track heard at half or double time. Each deck has key lock, cue and play, eight hot cues, loops and beat jumps, slip mode and a pitch fader, with stacked colour waveforms, beat grids and a phase meter.
- **Mixer.** Trim, a three-band EQ with kills and a filter on each channel, channel faders with meters, a crossfader with assign, curve, cut and reverse, and the master level, with 0 dB at 12 o'clock like a DJ mixer.
- **Scratching that feels like a record.** A jog wheel sets the record's speed from how fast its ticks arrive, very quick flicks included; a mouse drag follows the hand and a fast release throws the record. Touching a playing record catches it, and letting go ramps it back up.
- **DJ controllers.** Ready mappings for three popular controllers map both decks and the mixer at once, with LEDs that follow each deck. Any other controller maps through MIDI learn.
- **Performances you can edit.** While Gridline records, what you play becomes automation clips, one per control you used, kept together in a pattern that lands once on the playlist where you played it. Left as recorded, a pass replays exactly as played; cut it, trim it, move it or edit a curve, and playback can start anywhere in it.

See [docs/plugins/platter-recording.md](https://github.com/lolitemaultes/Gridline/blob/v0.7.0/docs/plugins/platter-recording.md).

### Seltmann, the automatic sample chopper

Drop audio on Seltmann and it works out what the sound is, its tempo and where bar 1 starts, chops it at a size that suits it into lettered regions, and plays as soon as you pick one of 59 layout cards. Breaks, melodic loops, whole songs, one-shots and textures all work.

- **The sequence lane** reorders, splits and joins regions, and the cut between two regions drags right in the waveform.
- **Pitch each region where it is**: drag the small readout on a pad or region, or turn the mouse wheel over it.
- **Start the sequence on any cell**: the cells before it move to the end, so the loop keeps its length.
- It plays as an instrument in time with your project, and the result goes out as a loop or a pattern.

See [docs/plugins/seltmann.md](https://github.com/lolitemaultes/Gridline/blob/v0.7.0/docs/plugins/seltmann.md).

### Plasma, a wavetable hybrid synth

Plasma has three oscillators (wavetable, multisample, sample, granular or spectral) with two warp slots each, unison up to 16 voices, a sub oscillator, a noise layer and two filters, 4 envelopes, 10 LFOs, 8 macros and a 64-slot modulation matrix (drag a source onto any knob), three effect racks, an arpeggiator, a clip player and a wavetable editor, in a calm editor that follows Gridline's theme.

Plasma also opens your own Serum 2 presets (`.SerumPreset`) and Serum `.fxp` files and turns them into Plasma sounds, with the wavetables, samples and other audio they carry. Its browser lists the preset folders found on your computer as a folder tree. Imported presets sound close to the original, and they get closer with each update. A project whose Serum or Kickstart is not installed plays it with Plasma or Tideline and keeps the original for a computer that has it. See [docs/plugins/plasma.md](https://github.com/lolitemaultes/Gridline/blob/v0.7.0/docs/plugins/plasma.md).

### Split stems

Right-click an audio clip and pick **Split stems...** to separate it into vocals, drums, bass and other; the six-stem model adds guitar and piano. The stems land on new tracks right under the clip, lined up with it and with its settings, in one undo step, or as files in the project's Stems folder. The split runs on your computer in the background, with progress and Cancel, and nothing is sent anywhere. A model is downloaded from its official address the first time you use it, after its licence note. See [docs/stems.md](https://github.com/lolitemaultes/Gridline/blob/v0.7.0/docs/stems.md).

### Import from URL

**File, Import, Import from URL...** (Ctrl+Shift+U) brings the audio of a web page into the project: paste a link, check the title and length, keep a range if you like, and the audio arrives as a lossless 32-bit float WAV clip on a new track at the playhead. The packages carry the programs it needs. Import only media you have the rights to use: Gridline does not sign in to sites or get around copy protection. See [docs/import-url.md](https://github.com/lolitemaultes/Gridline/blob/v0.7.0/docs/import-url.md).

### And much more

- **Kerf**, the slicer, is laid out like a full region slicer: a large wave view with numbered, named region flags, a region list, a deck bar with menus, a pad keyboard and calm cards. It slices on the beat grid (bars, beats, eighths, sixteenths, thirty-seconds and triplets) with Seltmann's beat analysis, and it has a bars and beats ruler, marker snapping and exact marker positions.
- **Pixel Tone** plays your own pictures: load or drop a PNG, JPEG or other picture and hear it.
- **Audio clips follow the tempo.** A stretched clip keeps its bars when the tempo changes, also through tempo automation; a clip you never stretched keeps its audio and its border grows or shrinks.
- **Smooth motion.** Zooming glides in the playlist, piano roll and automation editor, and smooth snapping can be seen and keeps up with fast drags.
- **A welcome screen** with your recent projects, favourites, templates and the demo song, and a **loading window** that opens big projects without freezing, with a log of what loaded and Cancel.
- **Automation clips inside patterns**, which play wherever the pattern plays.
- **One smooth stretch and pitch engine** for all audio, with Rubber Band as a third time mode for clips, the sampler and the audio editor.
- **Windows on every monitor**, every icon an SVG, **five control styles** with Slate as the default, **sound themes**, one undo step per gesture in every plugin editor, a redesigned plugin manager, and a new demo song, "Gridline".
- **Play lights** on every channel rack row, a one-click **route to a free mixer track** in every channel window, a slip tool that slips every selected clip, and a tempo display that steps whole BPM or fine-tunes the decimals.
- **FL Studio projects** open with their whole arrangement, also from its 2026 version: Slicex chops arrive in Kerf exactly, channels that stretch to a set time keep their length, and projects save back with their chops and plugin settings.
- **On Linux** the update popup downloads your distribution's own package, the `.deb` or the `.rpm`.

The complete list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/lolitemaultes/Gridline/blob/v0.7.0/CHANGELOG.md).

## Known limits

- Imported presets from other synths sound close but not always identical; this improves in updates.
- Collaborative projects use Gridline's own plugins only, and everyone in a session needs the same Gridline version: 0.7.0.
- Half-time grooves may still read at half speed in Platter and Seltmann; the /2 and x2 buttons put them right.
- Notes played live on a Rubber Band instrument start the moment you play them, so their start-up work is not spread out ahead.
- Split stems needs about 2 to 3 GB of memory while it runs, and its models are downloaded once, on request.
- On FreeBSD, OpenBSD and NetBSD, Import from URL uses the system's yt-dlp and ffmpeg packages.

## Upgrading

**From 0.6.x**

- Projects open as they are. Projects saved with the older stretch modes open with Stretch, and audio clips whose pitch was changed fill their clip again.
- The Gridline Suite and Gridline Suite Mini are new: the Windows installer and the Linux packages install them, and on macOS, with the AppImage and with the `.tar.gz` Gridline asks once whether to copy them into your plug-in folders.
- Gridline now starts on the welcome screen; turn off **Show the welcome screen** in **Settings, General, On startup** to start in a project.

**From a 0.7.0 beta**

- Projects open as they are. Pattern automation lanes open as automation clips inside their patterns, and Platter takes and passes recorded in the betas keep playing. Tracks analysed by Platter or Seltmann in beta 3 or earlier are analysed again.
- Installing 0.7.0 replaces the Gridline Suite copies in your plug-in folders at the first start, so they match this version.
- Plasma projects made in the betas: Hyper, the distorting filter types, the X-Shaper, the spectral oscillators, the arpeggiator, the flanger, the phaser, the delay, the compressor and the reverb now follow new laws, so projects that use them sound different.
- Collaborative projects: everyone in a session updates to 0.7.0.
- You can untick **Include beta versions** in **Settings, General, Updates**; the betas that follow 0.7.0 are offered only with it ticked.

## Install

| System | File |
| --- | --- |
| macOS 11 or later (Apple Silicon and Intel) | `Gridline-0.7.0-macos-universal.dmg` |
| Windows 10 / 11, x64 | `Gridline-0.7.0-windows-x64-setup.exe` or `-windows-x64.zip` |
| Windows 10 / 11, ARM64 | `Gridline-0.7.0-windows-arm64-setup.exe` or `-windows-arm64.zip` |
| Linux x86_64 | `.AppImage`, `-linux-amd64.deb`, `-linux-x86_64.rpm` or `-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |
| Linux aarch64 | `.AppImage`, `-linux-arm64.deb`, `-linux-aarch64.rpm` or `-linux-aarch64.tar.gz` |
| FreeBSD, OpenBSD, NetBSD (x86_64) | `-freebsd-x86_64.tar.gz`, `-openbsd-x86_64.tar.gz`, `-netbsd-x86_64.tar.gz` |

- **macOS:** open the `.dmg` and drag Gridline to Applications. The first time, open it with right-click, then Open.
- **Windows:** run the `-setup.exe`. If SmartScreen appears, choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
- **Linux:** use the `.AppImage`, `.deb`, `.rpm` or `.tar.gz`.
- **BSDs:** use the `.tar.gz` for your system.

The `Gridline-0.7.0-media-tools-<system>.zip` files hold FFmpeg for Import from URL. You do not need to download them: the packages already carry it, and a Gridline built from source fetches the one for its system the first time Import from URL is used, checked against the checksums below.

## Checksums

Every file's SHA-256 checksum is in `Gridline-0.7.0-SHA256SUMS`. Check a download with `sha256sum -c Gridline-0.7.0-SHA256SUMS --ignore-missing` (Linux, BSD), `shasum -a 256 -c Gridline-0.7.0-SHA256SUMS --ignore-missing` (macOS) or `Get-FileHash <file>` (Windows PowerShell). Gridline's update popup checks the checksum of what it downloads by itself.
