**Gridline 0.7.0 beta 3 is a beta.** It is published as a GitHub Pre-release, so Gridline's update check only offers it to people who turn on **Include beta versions** in **Settings, General, Updates**. Anyone can download it from this page, and 0.7.0 itself follows later. Projects from 0.6.1 and the earlier betas open as they are. If something does not work as it should, please [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/lolitemaultes/Gridline/issues); every report helps get 0.7.0 ready.

Beta 3 brings **Platter**, a two-deck DJ setup that lives on a channel, with performances you can record, see and edit. Gridline now starts on a **welcome screen** with your projects, favourites and a Collaborative view that shows who is working right now. All audio stretching and pitching runs through **one smooth engine**, with **Rubber Band** as a choice for clips. The **plugin manager** is redesigned, and many smaller things are better. Collaborative projects need everyone on the same version, so for this beta everyone in a session runs 0.7.0 beta 3.

## Highlights

### Platter: two decks on a channel

Platter is a whole DJ setup as an instrument: deck A and deck B with a mixer between them.

- **Decks.** Drop a track on a deck and Platter finds its tempo and beat grid, then plays it in time with the project or with the other deck. The padlock frees a deck to its own tempo. Each deck has key lock, cue and play, eight hot cues, loops and beat jumps, slip mode and a pitch fader, with stacked colour waveforms, beat grids and a phase meter.
- **Drag and drop.** Drag audio from the browser, your desktop or the playlist's clip list onto deck A or deck B, and it loads on the deck you dropped it on. Only the deck under the pointer lights up while you drag.
- **Mixer.** Trim, a three-band EQ with kills and a filter on each channel, channel faders with meters, a crossfader with assign, curve, cut and reverse, and the master level. The EQ and master knobs now have 0 dB at 12 o'clock, like a DJ mixer, so a mixer set by eye no longer sounds far too quiet.
- **Scratching that feels like a record.** The scratch mechanics follow the model Mixxx uses: a jog wheel sets the record's speed from how fast its ticks arrive, a mouse drag follows the hand and a fast release throws the record. Touching a playing record catches it, letting go ramps it back up, and Jog and Hand feel knobs set how it responds.
- **DJ controllers.** On first open, Platter asks whether you use a controller. Ready mappings for the Pioneer DDJ-200, Hercules DJControl Inpulse 200 and Numark Mixtrack Pro FX map both decks and the mixer at once, with LEDs that follow each deck. Without one, map your own controls with MIDI learn. Mappings are saved with the project and as presets.
- **Recorded performances you can edit.** While Gridline records, what you play on Platter becomes pattern data: every control you move becomes an automation lane, every button press (play, cue, hot cues, loops, beat jumps) a note on a named key in the piano roll, and scratches lanes for the mouse hand, the jog wheel and nudges. You see exactly when each thing happens, and editing the notes and lanes changes the sound. Left as recorded, it replays exactly as you played it, at any buffer size. A second pass never records over the first: it goes into a new pattern, and loop recording makes one pattern per pass. **Record as: Take** keeps a performance as one note instead, and takes recorded earlier keep playing.

### A welcome screen

Gridline now opens on a welcome screen, and a click on the Gridline logo brings it back at any time.

- **Your projects** in a large grid, each tile with a sketch of the arrangement, when it was last opened, its tempo and its length. The first tile starts a new project.
- **Favourites.** The heart on a tile (or F) keeps a project at hand; favourites are never pushed off the list.
- **Templates and the demo song**, opening from disk, search and tidying the list, all from the keyboard as well as the mouse.
- **The Collaborative view** lists every collaborative project on this computer with its members, your role and what is waiting for you, and shows who is working on each one right now. Presence is checked directly with your collaborators, encrypted, with no server.

To start straight in a project instead, turn off **Show the welcome screen** in **Settings, General, On startup**.

### One smooth stretch and pitch engine

- **One engine for all audio.** Audio clips, the sampler, slices, the audio editor, the Pitch Shifter, Platter and the other pitch and time effects all use the same continuous stretch. Every picker offers **Stretch** (time and pitch apart) and **Resample** (pitch and speed together, like a turntable).
- **Smoother sound.** Far fewer clicks and glitches from half speed to four times, no aliasing on pitched material, steadier bass, quieter noise around held notes, and drum hits without pre-echo. Live playback sounds the same as a render at any buffer size.
- **Rubber Band** is a third choice for clips, the sampler and the audio editor. It keeps drums and transients sharper, at a higher CPU cost.
- Projects saved with the older stretch modes open with Stretch.

### A new plugin manager

- **One row per plugin**, with the formats it is installed in as tags, a clean category, a star for favourites and a sortable table.
- **A preferred format**, or one per plugin: the browser and the plugin picker list each plugin once, in that format.
- **A details panel** with every format's version and location, scan problems with retry, the sandbox switch, a category override and Add to project.

### Smaller changes

- **Playlist:**
  - with several audio clips selected, a change to one clip's level, fades or pitch moves every selected clip by the same amount, and one drag is one undo step;
  - a right-click on a clip's title strip deletes it, like a right-click on its body.
- **Seltmann:**
  - a new Sequence lane plays the regions in order and lets you reorder them, split a region into equal parts and join it to its neighbour;
  - the editor is a quarter shorter, in Gridline's look, with calmer region colours.
- **Big plugin windows stay inside Gridline.** Platter, Seltmann, Plasma and the other wide editors no longer pop out on their own, and a window larger than the space it has scrolls instead of running off the edge.
- **The OUT meter** in the top bar is now a real master output meter, with a peak hold and a clip lamp that lights only above 0 dBFS. Meters fall more calmly and no longer show a peak marker in silence.
- The "Song sketch" project template is gone.

The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/lolitemaultes/Gridline/blob/v0.7.0-beta.3/CHANGELOG.md).

## Install

Install steps are the same as for the earlier betas:

- **macOS:** open the `.dmg` and drag Gridline to Applications. The first time, open it with right-click, then Open.
- **Windows:** run the `-setup.exe`. If SmartScreen appears, choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
- **Linux:** use the `.AppImage`, `.deb`, `.rpm` or `.tar.gz`.
- **BSDs:** use the `.tar.gz` for your system.

Every file's checksum is in `Gridline-0.7.0-beta.3-SHA256SUMS`.
